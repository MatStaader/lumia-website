Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024 | Version 1.0

TL;DR: We don't collect your data.

Lumia Bots run entirely on your hardware. No message content, user data, or server data ever leaves your machine. No analytics, no tracking, no external APIs for AI processing.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

We (Lumia developers) do not receive, store, or process:

  • Message content, DMs, or voice audio
  • User IDs, usernames, avatars, or profiles
  • Server names, member lists, roles, or permissions
  • Meeting transcripts, tasks, or knowledge base content
  • Usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry
  • IP addresses, device info, or location data

2. How the Bots Work (Locally)

All AI processing runs on your hardware via Ollama:

BotModels (Local)Data Stored Locally
TL;DRWhisper large-v3 (transcription), Llama-3.1-8B (extraction)SQLite: tasks, meetings; Temp: audio files
SentinelLlama-3.1-8B (auto-mod, FAQ, raid analysis)SQLite: config, FAQ, logs; ChromaDB: embeddings (Pro)

3. Data Storage

  • SQLite databases — Tasks, config, FAQ, logs (on your disk)
  • ChromaDB — Vector embeddings for semantic search (on your disk)
  • Temporary files — Audio recordings, deleted after processing
  • No cloud databases — Zero external data persistence

4. What We (Developers) See

Only aggregated, anonymous data visible in our Stripe dashboard:

  • Subscription status (active/cancelled)
  • Plan tier (Pro/Enterprise)
  • Billing email (for receipts)

We never see: server names, member counts, message content, or any Discord data.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local bot data: You control it. Remove bot = data stays on your disk until you delete it
  • Stripe data: Retained per Stripe's policy (financial records, typically 7+ years for tax)
  • Account deletion: Email us to request Stripe data removal (where legally permissible)

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request copy of data Stripe holds on you
  • Rectification: Update billing email via Stripe portal
  • Erasure: Request deletion (where not legally required to retain)
  • Portability: Export your local bot data anytime (SQLite/JSON)
  • Object: Opt-out of optional analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

8. Children's Privacy

Bots not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect children's data. Discord requires 13+.

9. Security

  • All bot-to-Discord communication over TLS (Discord API)
  • Website served over HTTPS (Cloudflare)
  • Payment data never touches our servers (Stripe Elements)
  • Local databases: your OS permissions control access

10. Changes to This Policy

Updates posted here and announced in support server. Material changes emailed (if billing email provided).
